## Engine equivalence (spec v0.3 amendment, change 4)

The cached engine was verified against uncached absolute-position references before the seal. Tolerances and observed maxima:

- Next-token logits, cached step against full forward: tolerance 1e-3; observed maximum difference 8.106e-6 in the crate battery's direct step-logits test (64 positions, run locally in release mode on the seal head's tree, 2026-11-10), and 6.437e-6 in the bench cross-check at production shape.
- Generated byte streams: identical between the cached engine and the inline absolute reference, across the battery's prompts and seeds (two seeds after the checkpoint fix).
- Generation NLL: agreement to a tight relative tolerance of 1e-5; observed maximum movement 6.7e-7 absolute on values near 4 to 5 (relative about 1.4e-7).
- Prompt gaps end to end: tolerance 0.25; observed maximum movement about 8.3e-5 (Appended 1.7800084 against 1.7799270, Control 17.1300506 against 17.1299681, Prepended minus 54.2519633 against minus 54.2520382).
- Capability block rule: cached 780.3541923848971 against inline 780.3542353739406 on the battery document, relative agreement about 5.5e-8, scored positions exactly 264 by construction (255 in the full block plus 9 in the trailing block).
- Cached step cost at production shape: about 9.85 s for a 100-token, 3-generation prompt on the detached model (10.45 s on the earlier VarMap build). The uncached side of that comparison swung with box load (361 s to 666 s), so no speedup ratio is recorded; the step cost and the logit equivalence are the stable figures.

## Dress rehearsal projection

Before picture (recency frame, spec v0.2): the old rehearsal banked 17 prompt timings before it was stopped, mean 1,586 seconds per prompt, projecting roughly 101 days of evaluation across the ladder and about 114 days in total. That projection is what the amendment's decision record rests on.

After picture (absolute positions, spec v0.3): MEASURED 2026-11-11. Method, stated on its face: no single rehearsal attempt survived the box's restart cadence long enough to print its verdict line, so the projection is computed from measurements taken by the driver's own code paths on the driver's own inputs. Prompt side: the amended rehearsal's streamed timing bank, 1,237 lines across watchdog relaunches, covering 209 distinct prompts (indices 0 to 208); per-prompt cost is the first-occurrence mean, 48.15 seconds (median 41.82, standard deviation 31.77); later prompts cost less on average than early ones, so extending this mean to all 300 prompts is conservative. Capability side: a standalone harness outside the sealed crate, timing the driver's own document_perplexities_cached over the driver's own capability documents (held-out documents 300 to 324 of the same 500-document cut) on a seed-initialised model built exactly as the rehearsal builds its evaluation model, one untimed warm-up document first; measured mean 31.31 seconds per document over 25 documents, projecting 6,262 seconds (1.74 hours) for the 200-document capability phase. Per-run evaluation is therefore 300 times 48.15 plus 200 times 31.31 = 20,707 seconds (5.75 hours); the 21-run ladder's evaluation totals 5.03 days; training is 335,978,496 tokens at the measured 296 tokens per second = 13.14 days. Grand total 18.17 days against the amendment's 21-day envelope for this experiment. Verdict: INSIDE the envelope. The re-sizing rules are not triggered and no further amendment is needed.
